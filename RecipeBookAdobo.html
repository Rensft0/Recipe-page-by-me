Adobo

Adobo is a popular filipino delicacy and aromatic smell to it

Recipe

  • chicken or pork
  • oil (any kind of oil)
  • bay leaves
  • laurel or pepper balls
  • pineapple
  • soy sauce
  • oyster sauce - optional
  • vinegar

Procedure

  • Prepare all ingredients (meat, garlic, onion, soy sauce, vinegar, bay leaves, peppercorns, oil, salt, and sugar).
  • Combine meat, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns in a bowl.
  • Mix well and marinate for at least 30 minutes or overnight.
  • Heat cooking oil in a pan.
  • Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  • Add the marinated meat and cook until lightly browned.
  • Pour in the remaining marinade and water.
  • Bring to a boil.
  • Lower the heat and simmer for 30–45 minutes or until meat is tender.
  • Add vinegar (do not stir).
  • Let it simmer uncovered for 5 minutes.
  • Stir and continue cooking until sauce thickens.
  • Season with salt and sugar to taste.
  • Serve hot with steamed rice.
