Pancit Canton

Pancit Canton is a popular filipino Noodle delicacy served in birhtday partied and celebrations and it combines healthy fibers and carbs

Recipe

ingredients

  • canton noodles
  • chicken or pork
  • shrimp - optional
  • cabbage
  • carrots
  • green beans
  • soy sauce
  • oyster sauce
  • garlic
  • onion
  • chicken broth or water
  • oil (any kind of oil)

Procedure

  • Prepare all ingredients (noodles, meat, shrimp, vegetables, garlic, onion, soy sauce, oyster sauce, chicken broth or water, oil, salt, and pepper).
  • Heat oil in a large pan or wok over medium heat.
  • Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  • Add chicken or pork and cook until lightly browned.
  • Add shrimp (if using) and cook until pink.
  • Add vegetables and stir-fry for a few minutes until they start to soften.
  • Pour in soy sauce and oyster sauce, stirring to combine.
  • Add chicken broth or water and bring to a boil.
  • Add canton noodles and cook according to package instructions, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.
  • Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  • Once noodles are cooked and liquid is mostly absorbed, remove from heat.
  • Serve hot and enjoy!
